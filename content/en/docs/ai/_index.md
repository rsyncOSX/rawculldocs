RawCull uses local AI at two stages: fast CLIP indexing across the catalog, followed by SAM 3 and Qwen review of selected candidates. Ordinary browsing and rating do not require the optional models.

- [AI Step by Step](/docs/ai/aistepbystep/) is the practical guide: narrow down a catalog, choose finalists, and review them in each AI tab.
- [AI Analysis](/docs/ai/aianalysis/) explains model roles, catalog indexing, downloads, and licences.
- [AI Screenshots](/docs/screenshotsai/screenshotsai/) shows the controls and results for all three tabs.

For catalog grouping and search, start with [Similarity, Bursts, and Search](/docs/similarity/).

[Combined Review](/docs/ai/combined-review/) is a separate workspace that brings source, regional, and model evidence together for 1–8 finalists. Enable it in AI settings.
